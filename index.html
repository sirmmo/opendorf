OpenDorf

Grow a medieval realm one hex tile at a time.

  1. Lay tiles next to your realm. Roads, rivers and coastlines must line up: a road can't run into a field, and the sea can only meet the sea or a river mouth.
  2. Houses put their land to work. A house next to a forest becomes a lumbermill, next to fields a windmill, by a river a watermill, under a mountain a mine, and by the sea a watchtower. Bigger forests, fields, rivers and seas feed more of them.
  3. Villages grow. Three houses together dig a well. Then come a tavern (5), a market (7), a church (10) and finally a castle (14). A village with a lumbermill and a mine gets a blacksmith.
  4. Every building and royal charter brings new tiles. When the last tile is laid, your realm is complete.
Mouse · click to lay · R or right click to turn · drag to move · right drag to rotate the view · wheel to zoom
Touch · tap a spot, tap again to lay · ⟲ ⟳ to turn · drag, pinch and twist the view
Keys · Z undo · X discard · C center · Q/E rotate the view · WASD move
