OpenDorf

Grow a medieval realm one hex tile at a time.

  1. Lay tiles next to your realm. Roads and rivers must carry on, and a beach must run on into more beach or open water. Land may meet the sea as a low cliff, and rivers may flow into the sea.
  2. Houses put their land to work. A house next to a forest becomes a lumbermill, next to fields a windmill, by a river a watermill, under a mountain a mine, and by the sea a watchtower. Bigger forests, fields, rivers and seas feed more of them.
  3. Villages grow. Three houses together dig a well. Then come a tavern (5), a market (7), a church (10) and finally a castle (14). A village with a lumbermill and a mine gets a blacksmith.
  4. Ruins of an older realm turn up now and then. A house next to them becomes a dig site (one for the first ruin, then one more for every two). A village with a dig site and a tavern or market founds an Archaeological Society; once it also has a church, the Society opens an Academy.
  5. Every building and royal charter brings new tiles. When the last tile is laid, your realm is complete.
